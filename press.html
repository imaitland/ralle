Johns Hopkins University research staff begin unionization effort

February 4, 2026

Johns Hopkins research staff launched a unionization campaign through RALLE-UE, partnering with United Electrical, Radio & Machine Workers of America. The initiative targets over 1,000 eligible staff members, with hundreds signing union cards. The union's platform centers on higher compensation, transparent career advancement pathways, and enhanced job security.
